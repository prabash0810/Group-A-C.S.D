Future advancements

Features that could take DayWise beyond the current coursework prototype.

01

Cloud accounts and secure database

Replace browser-only storage with secure authentication and synchronized data across devices.

02

Calendar synchronization

Connect Google Calendar, Outlook, and university timetables, with automatic clash detection.

03

Smart planning assistant

Recommend realistic study blocks, break large assignments into tasks, and reorganize missed work.

04

Intelligent reminders

Send browser, email, or mobile notifications based on deadlines, travel time, and task priority.

05

Reports and insights

Show weekly productivity, work hours, study consistency, completed goals, and achievement trends.

06

Collaboration

Support shared study plans, group-project deadlines, event invitations, and optional tutor views.
